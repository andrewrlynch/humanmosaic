About The Human Mosaic

The Human Mosaic is a “This Week In”-style newsletter bringing you the cutting-edgiest research at the interface of human evolution and human health. Tracking how we change in our lifetime and, what those changes mean for our health, and how these changes ripple through populations and generations.
